# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Interactive desktop duck with window walking, petting, food and ball games.
- Classic, pixel and voxel models, fitted clothes and saved personal looks.
- Live English and Russian localization following Omarchy's language setting.
- Quiet hours, work mode, reduced motion and power profiles.
- Unified `duck-art` studio for custom models, clothing and outfit packs.
- Native Omarchy installation, documented removal and local-only pet memory.
